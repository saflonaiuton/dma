THE CLASSIC FILIPINO RULES

How to play Dama

12 men each. Light moves first on the 32 dark squares of the 8×8 board.

Sapilitang kain — captures are compulsory. When choices exist, take a line that captures the most pieces.

Men move forward, capture either way. Chain captures continue with the same piece; jumped men stay as blockers until the chain ends.

Lumilipad na dama — the flying king. It slides diagonally any distance and can capture from range. Promotion in a chain is immediate.

Win by capturing all opposing pieces or leaving them no legal move. Tabla after 40 turns with no capture or man advancing.
